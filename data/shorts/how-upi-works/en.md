### UPI: A Revolution in Digital Payments

- Simplicity ki Baat: Bas ek UPI ID aur aap payment ya money receive kar sakte hain.
- Link Multiple Accounts: Ek hi app se alag-alag bank accounts manage karein.
- 24/7 Service: Kabhi bhi, kahin bhi paise bhejo ya lo. Raat din, chhutti par bhi!
