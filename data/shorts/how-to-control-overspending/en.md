### Over spending kaise control kre?

- Budget banaye: Monthly expenses track kare.
- Impulse buys se bachne ke liye wishlist maintain kare.
- Cash use kare: Credit card se zyada spending hoti hai.
- Discounts aur offers ka sahi use samjhe.
- Savings account mein fixed amount monthly transfer kare.
