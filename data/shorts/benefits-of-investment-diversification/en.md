### Investments Mein Diversification Ke Benefits!

- Diversification matlab alag-alag tarah ke investments mein paisa lagana.
- Isse risk kam hota hai, kyunki sab investments ek saath down nahi hoti.
- Stocks, bonds, real estate, aur mutual funds mein diversify karein.
- Market ke utar-chadhav ka asar kam hota hai diversification se.
- Har investment ka alag goal aur risk, isliye diversify karna smart move hai.
