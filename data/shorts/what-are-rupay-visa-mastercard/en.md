### Rupay, Visa aur Mastercard - Kya Hai? 

- Rupay: Bharat sarkar dwara launch ki gayi domestic card scheme.
- Visa/Mastercard: Antarrashtriya card platforms.
- Rupay se local transactions saste; Mastercard/Visa global acceptability ke liye.
- Sabhi mein unique security features.
