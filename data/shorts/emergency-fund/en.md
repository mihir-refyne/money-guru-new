### The Importance of Building an Emergency Fund -

Having an emergency fund is like having a piggy bank for when something unexpected happens, like getting sick or your car breaking. 
This helps you pay for these things without borrowing money and getting into debt. Save money in a separate account for 3-6 months of living expenses to feel safe.
