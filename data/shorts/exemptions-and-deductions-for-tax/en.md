### Exemption and deductions for tax: What are they?


Exemptions and deductions are ways to reduce your taxable income and save on taxes. Exemptions are specific amounts not counted as income, like HRA. Deductions are expenses or investments that can be subtracted from your income, like LIC premiums, ELSS. They help lower your tax liability and increase your taxable income.
