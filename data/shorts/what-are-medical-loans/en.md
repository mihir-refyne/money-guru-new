### What are Medical Loans?

- Jab health problem aaye aur paise ki zarurat ho, medical loan madad karta hai.
- Bina kisi guarantee ke, hospital ke bills ka bhugtan karein.
- Doctor ke fees se lekar dawaiyon tak, sab cover hota hai.
- Aasan kishton (EMI) par wapas karein, bina kisi pareshani ke.
- Loan ki shartein aur byaaj (interest) samajh ke hi loan lein.
- Jaldi process se loan mile, takibeena deri aapka ilaaj shuru ho sake.
