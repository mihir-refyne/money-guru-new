### Higher Interest Savings Account: Money Market Accounts

Money market accounts are low-risk investments like savings accounts offered by banks, with higher interest rates than regular savings accounts. They offer limited check-writing and debit card transactions but require a higher minimum balance.  They are also insured up to Rs. 5 lakh, but the interest rate may change based on market conditions.
