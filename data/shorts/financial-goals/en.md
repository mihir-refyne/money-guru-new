### How can you save for your emergency fund?

Determine your specific financial goals, whether short-term or long-term.
Write down your goals and make them specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
Create a budget and track your expenses to help you achieve your goals.
Stay motivated by regularly reviewing and celebrating your progress.
