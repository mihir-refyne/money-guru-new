### What are Form 16 and Form 26AS?


Form 16 is a document issued by your employer that shows your salary, tax deductions, and the tax paid on your behalf. It is used for filing income tax returns. Form 26AS is a tax credit statement that displays all the taxes paid by you, including TDS, advance tax, etc. It helps in verifying tax liabilities and refunds.
