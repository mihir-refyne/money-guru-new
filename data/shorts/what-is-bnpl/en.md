### Is 'Buy Now, Pay Later' Changing the Face of Credit?

Buy Now, Pay Later' (BNPL) is transforming how we think about credit.
- It's all about buying goods instantly, paying in later installments.
- No interest if you stick to your payment schedule. Tempting, right?
- But hold on! Always use responsibly to dodge the debt trap.
- It's the new-age credit system shaking up the retail world!
