### What are the different types of exemptions?


Under the old tax regime in India, some common exemptions include house rent allowance (HRA), leave travel allowance (LTA), medical reimbursements, standard deduction, and deductions for specific investments like home loan interest, tuition fees, and life insurance premiums. These exemptions can help reduce your taxable income
