### Savings: Why Should it be Your First Financial Step?

You've heard it before: 'Start saving early'. But why?
- Saving money helps you prepare for emergencies.
- It also makes you financially independent and secure.
- Start small, even a little saved today can become big tomorrow.
- Remember, it's not just about how much you earn, but how much you save.
