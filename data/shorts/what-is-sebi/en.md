### What is SEBI and How does it safeguard your investments ?

SEBI is a regulatory body established by the Indian government to protect investors' interests and regulate the stock market. Its objectives include - 
- Preventing fraudulent practices
- Developing a code of conduct for financial intermediaries
- Maintaining a balance between statutory regulations and self-regulation. 
- SEBI's primary aim is to safeguard investors and ensure the safety of their investments.
