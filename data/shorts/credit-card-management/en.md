### Mastering Credit Card Management: Tips to Track Expenses and Repay Debt

To track expenses and repay credit cards, set a budget, monitor spending, and pay at least the minimum amount due on time. Pay the full balance to avoid interest and use automatic payments. Consider consolidating debt with a balance transfer to avoid high-interest rates.
