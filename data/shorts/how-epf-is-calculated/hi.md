### How is EPF calculated? Does the employer contribute?


EPF is calculated as a percentage of your basic salary and dearness allowance. Both you and your employer contribute to your EPF account. The employee contributes 12% of their salary, while the employer contributes an equal amount. The total contribution is then added to your EPF account, which grows over time and helps you save for retirement.
