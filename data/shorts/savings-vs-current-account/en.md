### Savings Account Vs. Current Account

- Savings Account: Personal savings ke liye; limited transactions, byaaj prapt hota hai.
- Current Account: Vyaparik uddeshyon ke liye; unlimited transactions, no byaaj.
- Savings Account gives safety net; Current Account flexibility deta hai.
- Dono mein digital banking suvidha.
