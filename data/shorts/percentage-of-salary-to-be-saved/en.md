### What Percentage of your salary should you save? Are you saving enough? Know it here -

Saving is extremely important for a secure future and to cover unforeseen expenses. A general rule of thumb is to save at least 20% of your income, but you can adjust this based on your needs. For example, Rakhi earns Rs 60,000 monthly, she saved Rs 12,000 every month. By the end of the year, she had Rs1.4 lakhs in her savings. It's important to create a budget and prioritize saving to build a healthy financial future.
