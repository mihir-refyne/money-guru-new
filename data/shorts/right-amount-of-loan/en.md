### What is the right amount of Personal loan one should take?

The right amount of personal loan to take depends on your financial situation, income, expenses, and purpose for the loan. It's important to only borrow what you can afford to repay, and to consider the interest rates and fees associated with the loan. Typically one should not take a personal loan of more than 15 times of their monthly salary.
