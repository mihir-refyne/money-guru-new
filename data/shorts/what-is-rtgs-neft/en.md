### RTGS & NEFT Samjho: Bank Transfers ka Funda

- RTGS ka matlab: Real Time Gross Settlement. Bina kisi delay ke badi amounts transfer karo.
- NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer. Choti ya badi koi bhi raashi, fixed intervals mein transfer.
- Dono methods trusted aur RBI dwara monitored hai. Safe aur reliable.
