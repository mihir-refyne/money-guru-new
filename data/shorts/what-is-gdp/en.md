### GDP kya hota hai?

- GDP, Gross Domestic Product, desh ki economic performance batata hai.
- Total value of goods and services produced within a country's borders.
- High GDP indicates a healthy economy.
- GDP growth affects jobs, investments aur overall welfare.
- Isko track karna helps in understanding the economic health of a nation.
