### Get a Better Credit Score: Easy Tips to Improve Your Credit Score!

- Pay your bills on time and keep your credit card balances low.
- Don't apply for too many loans or credit cards at once.
- Use credit cards responsibly over a long time to build a good credit score
- You can get a credit card using an existing fixed deposit and improve your credit score by using it responsibly.
