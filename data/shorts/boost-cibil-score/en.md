### Unlock the Secrets to Boosting Your Credit Score: Six Tips for a Healthier CIBIL Score!

- Make transactions on Refyne, your withdrawn amount is timely paid by your employer which boosts your Credit score.
- Pay all credit card bills and loan EMIs on time
- Keep credit card balances low and don't use up the entire credit limit
- Maintain a healthy credit mix of secured and unsecured loans
- Avoid applying for multiple loans or credit cards simultaneously
- Increase credit limit and credit history by using credit cards responsibly over a long period of time.
- Incase of no-credit card, One can always take a Credit card against an existing FD and increase your Cibil score by using it Responsibily.
