### Are you financially ready to buy a car? Know it here

The basic rule one should follow to buy a car is <b>20/4/10</b>. 20 here stands for the down payment.One should be ready with 20% of down payment of the on-road price of the car. 4 stands for loan tenure,it should not be beyond 4years. Nowadays banks offer loan for 7years to reduce the monthly EMI and to optimize the eligibility to give the car loan. 10 stands for all your expenses related to that car like EMI,Insurance,maintenance,fuel charges etc.
