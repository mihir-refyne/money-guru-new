### Learn better budgeting with Fixed Interest Loans 

A fixed interest loan has an interest rate that remains same throughout the term of the loan.  Your monthly payments also remain the same, providing a predictable payment schedule that makes budgeting easier. However, if interest rates drop, your interest will remain same and <b>{you may end up paying more interest over the life of the loan}</b>.
