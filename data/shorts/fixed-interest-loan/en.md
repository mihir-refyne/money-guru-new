### Learn better budgeting with Fixed Interest Loans 

A fixed interest loan has an interest rate that remains the same throughout the term of the loan. This means that your monthly payments will also remain the same, providing you with a predictable payment schedule that can make budgeting easier. However, if interest rates drop, you will not benefit from the lower rates, and  <b>you may end up paying more interest over the life of the loan</b>.
