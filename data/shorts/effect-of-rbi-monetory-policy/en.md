### RBI ki Monetary Policy: Aapke Finances par Asar!

- Repo & Reverse Repo: Ye rates badalte hain, isse loan aur savings ki byaaj dar mein parivartan hota hai.
- Financial Stability: RBI ki policies se arthik sthirta ko bhi sudhaarne ka prayas kiya jata hai.
- Consumer Protection: Customers ke rights aur interests ko surakshit rakhne mein bhi ye neeti sahayak hoti hai.
