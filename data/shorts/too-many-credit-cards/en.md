### Maximum number of credit cards you should have

It's suggested to have 2-3 credit cards and other types of credit like personal loans etc. Your credit score can be affected by how much credit you have and how much debt you have. If you have more than 3 cards, it might be tough to pay them all on time. Only get a new card if you can afford to pay it back.
