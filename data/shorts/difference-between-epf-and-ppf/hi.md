### What is the difference between EPF and PPF?


EPF is a retirement savings scheme where a portion of your salary is deducted and deposited into the EPF account, managed by EPFO. PPF is a long-term savings scheme offered by the government. EPF is contributed by the employee and employer, while PPF is a personal investment. EPF is tied to employment, while PPF is open to all.
