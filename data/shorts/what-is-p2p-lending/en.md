### Can P2P Lending Speed Up the Loan Process?

Peer-to-Peer (P2P) lending connects borrowers and lenders directly.
- Speed up the loan process, bypassing traditional banking.
- Better returns for lenders, attractive interest rates for borrowers.
- Sounds great, but remember, always evaluate the risk-return trade-off.
