### IPO se PAISE KAISE KAMAYE?

- Jab company pehli baar stock market mein shares bechti hai, use IPO kehte hain.
- Sahi company aur sahi samay par invest karke achha return paaya ja sakta hai.
- Lekin, har IPO mein invest karne se pehle research karna bahut zaroori hai.
- High returns ke liye thoda risk bhi lena pad sakta hai.
