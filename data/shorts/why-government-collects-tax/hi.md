### Why does the government collect income tax?


The government collects income tax to fund various public services and infrastructure. It helps in running the country, providing education, healthcare, roads, security, and other essential services. Income tax ensures equal distribution of wealth. It is important to pay income tax to support the progress and welfare of the country.
