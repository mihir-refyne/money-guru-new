### Jan Dhan Yojana kya hai?

- Zero balance account khulwao, koi charge nahi.
- Accident insurance ₹2 lakh tak muft.
- Overdraft suvidha ₹10,000 tak; financial freedom.
- RuPay card ke sath, har kadam pe banking.
