### What is investment? Earn extra money without working

Investment means putting money or resources into something with the expectation of making a profit or income in the future. It can take many forms, such as stocks, bonds, real estate, or mutual funds, and the goal is to grow wealth over time. <br>

For example, Seema invested Rs 10,000 in a company's stocks at Rs 100 each, totalling 100 stocks. A year later, she sold them for Rs 150 each, making a profit of Rs 5,000. This is an example of earning money through stock market investment.
