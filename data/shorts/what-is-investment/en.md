### What is investment? Earn extra money without working 

Investment means putting money into something like stocks, bonds, real estate, or mutual funds and expecting to make a profit or income. 

Seema invested Rs 10,000 in a company's stocks at Rs 100 each, totalling 100 stocks. A year later, she sold them for Rs 150 each, making a profit of Rs 5,000. An example of earning money through investment.
