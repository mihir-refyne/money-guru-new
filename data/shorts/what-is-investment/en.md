### What is investment? Make your money work for you.

Investment means putting money or resources into something with the expectation of making a profit or income in the future. It can take many forms, such as stocks, bonds, real estate, or mutual funds, and the goal is to grow wealth over time. Seema invested Rs 10,000 in a company's stocks at Rs 100 each, totaling 100 stocks. A year later, she sold them for Rs 150 each, making a profit of Rs 5,000. This is an example of making money through stock market investment.
