### Pehla Mutual Fund Kaise Kharidein?

- Short-term ya long-term, apne nivesh ke lakshya ko samjhein.
- Apne risk capacity ke hisaab se fund chune.
- Banks, brokers ya online apps se mutual fund mein nivesh karein.
- Mutual Fund khareedne se pehle KYC complete karna zaroori hai.
- Decide karein ki aapko har mahine thoda-thoda paisa daalna hai ya ek baar mein pura.
