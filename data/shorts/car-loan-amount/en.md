### What is the right amount of Car Loan one should take?

The right amount of car loan depends on your budget, income, and expenses. It's important to consider how much you can afford to pay each month and not borrow more than necessary. Typically the monthly payment of your loan should exceed 10-15% of your monthly income. Look for the best interest rates and loan terms to get the best deal.
