### What is a Reducing interest loan?What is a Reducing interest loan and how can it help you with better Saving on Interest?

A reducing-interest loan has an interest rate that is recalculated periodically based on the outstanding loan balance. As you make payments and reduce the principal, the interest charged on the outstanding balance decreases. <b>This means that you can save money on interest over the life of the loan</b>, but your monthly payments may vary, which makes budgeting more challenging.
