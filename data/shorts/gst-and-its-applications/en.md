### Understanding GST and its implications.


GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a single tax system that replaced multiple taxes like VAT, excise duty, and service tax. It is applied to the sale of goods and services. GST makes taxation simpler and transparent. It has different slabs for different items. It is important to understand GST to comply with tax regulations and ensure fair trade practices.
