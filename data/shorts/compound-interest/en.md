### Multiply your money with time: Power of Compound Interest.

Compound interest is when your money grows faster as you earn interest on the money you already earned. 
For example, if you start with Rs 100 and earn 8% interest, you'll have Rs 108 after a year. Then, you'll earn interest on the Rs 108 instead of just the initial Rs 100, and your savings will grow faster and faster over time.
