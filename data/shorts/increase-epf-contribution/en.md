### Can I contribute more than the mandatory EPF amount?


Yes, you have the option to contribute more than the mandatory EPF amount. It is called Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF). By contributing more, you can increase your retirement savings and enjoy higher returns. It is a great way to secure a better financial future for yourself and your family.
