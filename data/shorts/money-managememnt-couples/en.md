### Money Management for Couples: How to save money together?

Managing money as a couple means working together to handle income, expenses, and savings. Talk about financial goals and create a budget. Keep track of spending and consider sharing bank accounts. Decide how to split expenses fairly. Check in regularly to make sure you're both on track for a secure financial future.
