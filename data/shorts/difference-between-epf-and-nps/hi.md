### NPS vs EPF: Which is better?


NPS (National Pension System) and EPF (Employee Provident Fund) are both retirement savings options. NPS is a voluntary scheme where you can contribute for pension benefits. EPF is mandatory and deducted from your salary. NPS provides potential higher returns but involves market risk. EPF offers stable returns and is secure.
