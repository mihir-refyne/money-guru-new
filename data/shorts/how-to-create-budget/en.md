### How to Create Your Budget?

Creating a budget involves:
- Income Estimation: Tally all income sources.
- Expense Breakdown: List all monthly expenses, both fixed and variable.
- Allocate Funds: Distribute your income into different categories like necessities, savings, and leisure.
- Adjust and Balance: Ensure that your expenses don't exceed your income. If they do, cut back on non-essentials.
- Review regularly and adjust as financial situations change.
