### Ghar Khareede Ya Kiraye Pe Le?

- Owning gives long-term security; rent offers flexibility.
- EMI vs Rent: Kya aapke budget ke andar hai?
- Maintenance: Own home mein aap responsible; rent mein landlord help karte hain.
- Resale value: Ghar value badh sakti hai over time.
- Location: Rent mein aapko flexibility to relocate.
