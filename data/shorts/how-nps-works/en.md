### How does NPS work?


NPS is a retirement savings scheme where you contribute a portion of your income regularly. It is managed by the government and invests your money in various financial instruments. The money grows over time and you can withdraw a portion at retirement. It helps you save for a secure future and enjoy a comfortable retirement.
