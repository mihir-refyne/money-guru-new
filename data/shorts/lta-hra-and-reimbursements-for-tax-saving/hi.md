### How do LTA, HRAs, and reimbursements help in tax saving?


LTA (Leave Travel Allowance) is for travel expenses during leave, HRAs (House Rent Allowance) help with house rent, and reimbursements cover expenses related to work. They are helpful for tax saving as they are exempt from tax up to certain limits. By using these allowances, you can reduce your taxable income and save on taxes.
