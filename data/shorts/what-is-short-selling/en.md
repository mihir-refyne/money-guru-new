### Short Selling kya hai?

- Shares ko high price pe sell karo, baad mein low price pe buy back karo.
- Isse profit hota hai jab market price decrease hoti hai.
- High risk involved; research zaroori hai.
- Best suited for experienced investors.
- Market knowledge aur strategy ke bina mat try karo.
