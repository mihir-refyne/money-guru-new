### How to save more by dividing your salary efficiently?

The 50/30/20 rule is a general guideline for allocating your monthly salary. 
- 50% for essential needs like food, rent, and groceries
- 30% for discretionary spending like eating out and hobbies
- 20% for savings and debt repayment. Adjust according to your individual needs and goals, and regularly review and update your budget.
