### Loan Chukane Ke Strategies

- Snowball Method: Chhote loans pehle chukayein, fir bade loans ki taraf badhein. Yeh method aapko motivation deti hai.
- Avalanche Method: Jis loan par sabse jyada byaj hai, usse pehle chukayein. Long term mein aapka paisa bachata hai.
- Budgeting: Apne kharche kam karein aur loan repayment ke liye zyada budget banayein. Yeh aapko karz mukt hone mein tezi se madad karega.
