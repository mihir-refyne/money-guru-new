### BHIM UPI kya hai?

- BHIM UPI: Direct bank-to-bank transfer using mobile.
- Benefits: Fast, 24x7, no need for account number.
- Safety: Unique UPI PIN for secure transactions.
- Takeaway: BHIM UPI se easy & secure payments!
