### How to Start Budgeting?

- Assess Your Income: Calculate your monthly income, including salary, bonuses, and other sources.
- List Expenses: Categorize them as fixed (rent, EMI) or variable (dining, entertainment).
- Set Financial Goals: Whether it's buying a home or saving for vacations.
- Choose a Budgeting Method: Such as envelope system or 50/30/20 rule.
- Start by tracking every rupee spent to understand your spending habits.
