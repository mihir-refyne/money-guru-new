### Credit Card Loan vs Personal Loan: Kya Hai Behtar?

Credit Card Loan:
- Instant access ke liye achha.
- Chhote expenses ya short-term needs ke liye suitable.
- Ucch interest rates ho sakte hain.

Personal Loan:
- Bade kharchon ke liye behtar, jaise ghar ki marammat, shaadi, ya education.
- Interest rate aksar kam hota hai credit card loans ki tulna mein.
- Repayment period zyada flexible aur long-term hota hai.

Apne jarurat aur repayment capacity ke hisab se sahi loan chunein.
