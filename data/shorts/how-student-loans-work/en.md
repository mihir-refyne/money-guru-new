### Student loans kaise kaam krte hai?

- Loan amount basis: College fees aur living expenses.
- Interest rates: Fixed ya variable hota hai.
- Repayment: Studies complete hone ke baad start hoti hai.
- Deferment options: Financial hardship case mein possible.
- Forgiveness programs: Certain jobs ya locations mein available.
