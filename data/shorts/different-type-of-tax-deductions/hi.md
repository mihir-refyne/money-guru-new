### What are the different types of deductions?


Deductions in old tax regime:

- 80C: PF, Life Insurance Premium, NSC
- 80D: Health Insurance Premium
- 80G: Donations
- 80TTA: Savings account interest
- 80E: Education loan interest
Lower taxes, more savings.
