### GST kya hai?

- GST (Goods & Services Tax): Har product/service pe lagne wala tax.
- Benefits: Ek unified tax, reduce 'tax-on-tax'.
- Know: Different slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%.
- Takeaway: GST se better tax system & transparency!
