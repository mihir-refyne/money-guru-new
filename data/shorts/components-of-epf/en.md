### What are the components of EPF?


The three components of EPF are:

- Employee contribution: A portion of your salary is deducted each month and contributed towards EPF.
- Employer contribution: Your employer also contributes an equal amount to your EPF account.
- Interest: The EPF balance earns interest annually, helping it grow over time.
