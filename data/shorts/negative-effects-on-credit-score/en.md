### Things that will have a negative effect on your cibil score. Stay aware!

- Late payments and defaults on loans or credit card bills
- High credit utilization ratio or maxing out credit cards
- Settlements or write-offs of loans or credit cards
- Multiple loan or credit card applications in a short period of time
- Lack of credit history or credit mix diversity
- Errors in credit reports or fraudulent activity on accounts.
