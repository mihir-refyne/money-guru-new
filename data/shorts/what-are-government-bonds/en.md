### Government BONDS kya hote hai?

Government bonds ek tarah ke loan hote hai jise sarkar aam logon se leti hai, promise karte huye ki ek nirdharit samay par principal amount wapas karegi, saath hi ek fixed interest rate par byaaj bhi degi. Yeh bonds risk-free maane jaate hain kyunki inhe sarkar back karta hai. Yadi aap safe investment pasand karte hain, toh government bonds aapke liye accha option ho sakte hain.
