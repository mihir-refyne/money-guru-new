### TDS and Advance Tax: What are they?


TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is when a portion of your income is deducted by the payer and submitted to the government as tax. Advance Tax is when you pay your income tax in installments throughout the year, based on estimated income. TDS is deducted by others on your behalf, while Advance Tax is paid by you directly to the government.
