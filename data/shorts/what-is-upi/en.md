### UPI Payments kya hai?

- UPI: Instant money transfer via mobile.
- Bank account link karke, 24x7 payment possible.
- Aadhar, mobile, QR code se payment options.
- Safe & fast; ₹1 se ₹2 lakh tak ka transaction.
