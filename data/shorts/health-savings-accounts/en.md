### How to save money on your medical bills? 

A Health Savings Account (HSA) is a special savings account for medical expenses. You can put money into it before taxes, and it grows tax-free. You can use the money in the account to pay for medical expenses like doctor visits and medicine. Only people with a high-deductible health plan can have an HSA.
