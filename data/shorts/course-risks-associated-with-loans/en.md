### Loan Ke Risks: Samjhe Aur Sambhale

- Byaj Dar: Loan lene se pahle, byaj dar samajhna zaroori hai. Jyada byaj aapki financial sthiti ko prabhavit kar sakta hai.
- Chukauti: Loan ka samay aur raashi tay kar le, takki monthly budget par bhaari na pade.
- Karz Ka Jaal: Multiple loans se bach ke rahiye; yeh aapko karz ke jaal mein phasa sakta hai.
