### Study Now, Pay Later: Education Loans Made Easy

Education loans reduce financial strain on families and eliminate the need to liquidate investments. They offer tax rebates and flexible repayment options.  Students can pay for their own education and build their credit profile early. EMIs start after one year of graduation, and a good credit score can help get cheaper loans in the future.
