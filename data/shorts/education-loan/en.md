### Educations Loans : How you can finance your education in your dream college

Education loans reduce financial strain on families and eliminate the need to liquidate investments. They offer tax rebates and flexible repayment options. It's a great opportunity for students to pay for their own education and build their credit profile. EMIs start only after one year of graduation, and a good credit score can help get cheaper loans in the future.
