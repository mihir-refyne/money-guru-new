### Type of Loans

- Personal Loan: Har tarah ke kharche ke liye, bina security ke.
- Home Loan: Ghar khareedne ke liye, property against loan.
- Education Loan: Padhai ke liye, future salary se repay.
Every loan is different and has it's own nature, interest rate, aur eligibility criteria.
