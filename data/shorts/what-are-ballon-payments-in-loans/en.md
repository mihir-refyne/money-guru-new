### Loans mai Balloon Payments kya hote hai?

Balloon payment ek bada payment hota hai jo loan ke ant mein aata hai. Shuruat mein chote monthly payments, lekin loan ke end par ek bada lump sum payment.
- Pros: Kam monthly payments se aapke short-term finances par kam burden.
- Cons: End mein bade payment ki zaroorat. Yeh financial planning ke liye challenging ho sakta hai.
Is type ke loan lene se pehle apne long-term financial goals aur stability ko dhyan mein rakhein. Achanak ke bade kharche aapko pareshan kar sakte hain.
