### PPF Ka Magic (Public Provident Fund)

- PPF: 15 saal ki savings scheme.
- Lock-in period: 15 years; reinvest kar sakte hain.
- Tax-free interest; maturity par bhi tax nahi.
- ₹500 se ₹1.5 lakh tak har saal jama kar sakte hain.
- Retirement ke liye best option!
