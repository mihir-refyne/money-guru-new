### Fixed Deposit vs. Recurring Deposit

- Fixed Deposit (FD): Ek baar paisa daalo, earn karo high interest - Best for: Lump sum savings.
- Recurring Deposit (RD): Har month thoda paisa daalo - Best for: Regular savings.
- Takeaway: FD for lump sum; RD for monthly savings.
