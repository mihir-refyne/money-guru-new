### What is Budgeting?

Budgeting is the process of creating a financial plan that outlines your income and expenses for a specific period, usually monthly or annually. It's a tool that helps you:
- Manage your finances effectively.
- Prioritize essential expenses.
- Save for future goals and avoid overspending.
- By adhering to a budget, you can ensure financial stability and grow your wealth.
