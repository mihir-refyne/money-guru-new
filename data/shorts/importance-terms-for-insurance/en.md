### Important terms before buying Insurance!

- Premium: Jo amount aap policy ke liye pay karte hain.
- Deductible: Claim karne se pehle khud pay karna padta hai.
- Coverage: Kya-kya situations mein insurance company payment karegi.
- Exclusion: Policy mein covered nahi hone wali situations.
- Claim: Jab aap insurance company se payment maangte hain.
