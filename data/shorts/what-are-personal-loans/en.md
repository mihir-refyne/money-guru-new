### What are Personal Loans?

- Personal loan ek type ka unsecured loan hota hai, jis ke liye aapko koi collateral girvi rakhne ki avashyakta nahi hoti hai.
- Kisi bhi uddesh ke liye use kiya jaa sakta hai, jaise ki medical emergency, home renovation, wedding, ya koi anya bada kharch.
- Interest rate baaki loans se adhik hota hain.
- Personal loan ki EMI aapke masik budget par bojh nahi dalni chahiye.
