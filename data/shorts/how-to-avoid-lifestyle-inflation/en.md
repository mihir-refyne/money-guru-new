### Lifestyle inflation se kaise bache?

- Income badhne par bhi, kharche na badhayein - lifestyle inflation se bachein.
- Savings aur investments ko priority dein, har salary hike ke saath.
- Zarurat se zyada ke luxuries pe paise na barbaad karein.
- Long-term goals ke liye plan banayein, aur us par kayam rahein.
- Monthly budgeting ko na bhulein - isse aapko spending par control milega.
