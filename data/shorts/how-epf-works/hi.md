### How does EPF work?


EPF is like a savings account for your retirement. A portion of your salary is deducted every month and added to your EPF account. Your employer also contributes to this account. Over time, your EPF account grows with interest. When you retire, you can withdraw the money in your EPF account. It helps you save for a secure future.
