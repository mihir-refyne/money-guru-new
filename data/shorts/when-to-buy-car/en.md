### Are you financially ready to buy a car? Know it here 

The rule to follow when buying a car is <b>{20/4/10}</b>. 
20 - One should be ready with 20% of down payment of the on-road price of the car.
4 - loan tenure should not exceed 4 years. Banks offer loans for 7 years, reducing monthly EMI and optimising eligibility for a loan. 
10 - all car related expenses should not exceed 10% of your monthly salary.
