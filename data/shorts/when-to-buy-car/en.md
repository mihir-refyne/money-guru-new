### Are you financially ready to buy a car? Know it here

The basic rule one should follow to buy a car is <b>{20/4/10}</b>. 
20 - down payment. One should be ready with 20% of down payment of the on-road price of the car.
4 - loan tenure, it should not be beyond 4years. Nowadays banks offer loan for 7years to reduce the monthly EMI and to optimize the eligibility to give the car loan. 
10 - all your expenses related to the car, Expense should not exceed 10% of your inhand salary.
