### Currency Exchange ke important tips!

- Other countries jaate waqt currency exchange rates samjhein.
- Local currency ke liye commission aur fees dekh ke badlein.
- Forex cards ek smart choice ho sakti hai, extra charges se bachne ke liye.
- Airport par na badlein, rates jyada hote hain.
- Local banks ya authorised money changers ka use karein.
