### How is EPF different from VPF?


EPF (Employee Provident Fund) is a mandatory savings scheme where both you and your employer contribute. VPF (Voluntary Provident Fund) is an additional savings option where you can contribute more from your salary. EPF is compulsory, while VPF is optional but helps you save more for a secure future.
