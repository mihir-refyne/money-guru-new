### Credit Score Ki Importance

- Credit score aapke credit history ka summary hai, jo banks aur lenders dekhte hain loan approve karte waqt.
- High credit score se loan milna aasan hota hai aur interest rates bhi kam mil sakte hain.
- Regular bill payments aur smart financial decisions se aapka score improve hota hai. Kam credit score wale customers ke liye loan lena mushkil ho sakta hai.
