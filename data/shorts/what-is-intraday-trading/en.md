### Intraday trading kya hai?

- Intraday trading matlab ek hi din mein stock khareedna aur bechna.
- Fatafat profit kamane ka tarika hai, lekin risk bhi adhik hota hai.
- Market ke quick movements par najar rakhna zaroori hai.
- Technical analysis aur timely decision lene ki skill ki zarurat hoti hai.
