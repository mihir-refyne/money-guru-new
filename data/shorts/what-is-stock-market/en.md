### What is stock market and how you can invest in stocks and grow your money?

The stock market is a platform where publicly traded companies issue and sell shares to investors and common public. You can buy and sell these shares in hopes of making a profit as the company grows and becomes more valuable.

For example, you can buy shares of Apple stock and potentially make a profit if the company's stock price increases over time.
