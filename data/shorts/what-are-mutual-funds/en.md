### What are mutual funds? How to double your money over time

Mutual funds are investment vehicles that pool money from multiple investors to invest in a portfolio of stocks, bonds or other securities. They are managed by professional fund managers who aim to maximize returns for investors.

Sumit invested Rs 50,000 in a mutual fund. Over time, the value of Sumit's investment increased to over Rs 1 Lakh
