### What are mutual funds? How you can multiply your money time

Mutual funds are investment vehicles that pool money from multiple investors to invest in a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds or other securities. They are managed by professional fund managers who aim to maximize returns for investors. Sumit invested Rs 50,000 in a mutual fund that tracks the performance of the top 500 companies in India.Over time, the value of Sumit's investment increased to over Rs 1 Lakh
