### Are mutual funds right for you?

Mutual funds are investments that pool money from multiple investors in a portfolio of stocks, bonds or other securities. They are managed by fund managers who aim to maximize returns for the investors.

Sumit invested Rs 50,000 in a mutual fund. Over 5-6 years, the value of Sumit's investment increased to Rs 1 Lakh (Based on Market Risk)
