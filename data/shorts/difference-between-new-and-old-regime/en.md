### Old Regime vs New Regime: Which is preferable?


The Old Regime allows more deductions and exemptions, reducing taxable income. The New Regime has lower tax rates but limited deductions. If you have many deductions, the Old Regime may be better. If you prefer simplicity, the New Regime is preferable. It depends on your specific financial situation and preferences.
