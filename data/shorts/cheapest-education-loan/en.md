### Get the Best Deal: Tips for Finding the Cheapest Education Loan

To find the cheapest education loan, shop around, check for special offers, and consider government schemes. Collateral and co-signers can help lower interest rates, and evaluate repayment terms, such as flexible repayment periods or interest-only EMIs. Generally, Public banks offer the cheapest education loans
