### Samajhiye Loan kya hota hai!

- Loan ek prakar ka vittiya samjhauta hai, jahan ek vyakti ya sanstha (lender) dusre vyakti ko paise udaar deti hai.
- Paise udaar lene wala (borrower) is raashi ko wapas karne ka vaada deta hai, saath hi byaaj (interest) bhi deta hai.
- Loans ki vibhinn prakar hote hain, jaise personal loan, home loan, education loan, etc. Har loan ki apni shartein hoti hain, jaise repayment period, interest rate, aur loan amount.
