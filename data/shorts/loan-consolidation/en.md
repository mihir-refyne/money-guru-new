### Secret way of reducing your EMIs

Loan consolidation means paying off an old loan by taking a new loan with a lower interest rate, leading to lower monthly payments. It's a helpful option for those struggling to pay high EMIs. 

For Example - Sita saved Rs. 24,000 a year by consolidating her personal loan with a cheaper loan.
