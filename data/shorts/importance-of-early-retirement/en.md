### Jaan lo Early retirement ka masterplan!

- Early retirement matlab early planning.
- Invest karo stocks, mutual funds mein, aur create karo passive income.
- Budgeting se expenses control karo; extra paisa invest karo.
- Compounding ka magic samjho; time ke saath growth hoti hai.
- Always consult financial advisor before making big decisions.
