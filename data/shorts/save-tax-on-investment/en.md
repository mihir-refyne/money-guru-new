### Investment par TAX Kaise Bachaye?

- ELSS, PPF, aur NPS jaise schemes se tax bachaya ja sakta hai.
- Ye schemes sarkar dwaara approved hote hain.
- Inme invest karke tax ke benefits ke saath saath achhe returns bhi paaye ja sakte hain.
- Sahi nivesh ke liye pehle ache se samajhna aur research karna important hai.
