### Important points before taking a CAR loan!

- Car loan lene se pehle down payment kitna hoga, yeh dekhein.
- Interest rates aur loan ke term par research karein.
- EMI aapke monthly budget mein fit baithta hai ya nahi, check karein.
- Car ke resale value ka bhi dhyaan rakhein.
- Car insurance aur additional costs ka hisab lagayein.
- Loan ke liye apply karne se pehle saari offers compare karein.
