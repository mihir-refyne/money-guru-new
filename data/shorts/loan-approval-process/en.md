### Loan Approval Process kya hota hai?

- Application: Sabse pehle, loan ke liye apply karte hain. Bank ya lender aapke documents jaise income proof, identity proof check karta hai.
- Credit Check: Aapki credit history aur score ka evaluation hota hai. Agar aap criteria fulfill karte hain, toh loan approve hota hai, warna deny.
- Loan Terms: Loan approve hone par, byaaj dar aur repayment terms decide hote hain.
