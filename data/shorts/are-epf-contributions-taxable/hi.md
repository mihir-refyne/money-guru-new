### Are EPF contributions taxable?


No, EPF contributions are not taxable. The money you contribute to your EPF account is deducted from your salary before taxes, which means you don't have to pay taxes on that amount. However, the interest earned on EPF is taxable if withdrawn before completion of 5 years.
