### How to Create a Realistic Budget -

Creating a budget helps you manage your money. 
- Start by writing down what you make and what you spend. 
- Take out what you need to pay for important things, like food and housing. 

Then, decide how much you can spend on fun things and saving. Check your budget every month and change it if needed.
