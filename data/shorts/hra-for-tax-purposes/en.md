### Understanding HRA for tax purposes.


HRA (House Rent Allowance) can be beneficial for saving tax. If you live in a rented house and receive HRA as part of your salary, you can claim tax deductions. The amount of deduction depends on factors like HRA received, rent paid, and salary. Keep proper rent receipts and other necessary documents to avail these benefits.
