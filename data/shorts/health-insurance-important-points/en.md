### Health Insurance lene se pehle Yeh Points Yaad Rakhein!

- Coverage Type: Family ya individual coverage, kya chahiye?
- Sum Insured: Aapko kitna coverage chahiye?
- Waiting Period: Kuch bimaarion ka wait period hota hai.
- Claim Process: Cashless ya reimbursement?
- Hospital Network: Bima company ke network mein konsa hospital hai?
- No Claim Bonus: Agar claim nahi liya, to bonus milega!
