### Dangers of taking unofficial loans from moneylenders

Unofficial moneylenders use tactics like harassment, blackmailing, and violence to make you repay the money. They also add hidden charges, such as processing fees, which makes the loan more expensive than it seems. Also, they do not give you any legal documents or proof of the loan, making it difficult to prove that you have repaid the money.
