### Beware of Unofficial Loan Sharks: The Risks of Borrowing from Untrustworthy Sources.

Unofficial loan sharks may use tactics like threatening phone calls, harassment, blackmailing, or even violence to make you pay back the money. They may also add hidden charges, such as processing fees, which can make the loan more expensive than it seems. Additionally, they may not give you any legal documents or proof of the loan, making it difficult to prove that you have repaid the money.
