### Debt Snowball: Karz Chukane Ka Smart Tarika!

- Debt Snowball method se chhote loans pehle chukayein.
- Har loan chukane par confidence badhega.
- List bana kar smallest se biggest loan ki taraf badhein.
- Regular payments ke saath, chhote debts ko jaldi khatam karein.
- Bade loans ke liye motivation milega jab chhote loans clear ho jayein.
