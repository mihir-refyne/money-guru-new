### The Magic of Compound Interest

- Compound interest, ya "chakravarti byaaj," aapke investments ko tezi se badhata hai.
- Yeh interest on interest hai - matlab aapke pehle ke byaaj par bhi byaaj milta hai.
- Long term me, yeh simple interest se kahin zyada faydemand hota hai.
- Regularly invest karna aur samay dena iska key secret hai.
- Retirement funds, FDs, aur savings accounts me iska use hota hai.
- Jitna jaldi shuru karenge, utna adhik labh hoga - shuru karein aur jadu dekhein!
