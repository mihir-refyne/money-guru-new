### How to Get Loans for Small Businesses and Startups

There are several government schemes for small businesses such as the Mudra Loan Scheme, Stand-Up India Scheme, and PMMY.

Apply for these by visiting the official website of the schemes and submitting the required documents. These schemes provide loans at great rates provided by various financial institutions like banks, NBFCs, MFIs, etc.
