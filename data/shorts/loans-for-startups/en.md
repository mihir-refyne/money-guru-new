### Unlocking Government Funds: How to Get Loans for Small Businesses and Startups

There are several government schemes to get loans for small businesses or starting a business, such as the Mudra Loan Scheme, Stand-Up India Scheme, and PMMY.

You can apply for these loans by visiting the official website of the respective scheme and submitting the required documents. These schemes provide loans at great rates and are provided by various financial institutions like banks, NBFCs, MFIs, etc.

One can also apply as Startup India certificate for unique ideas and apply through it for small loans
