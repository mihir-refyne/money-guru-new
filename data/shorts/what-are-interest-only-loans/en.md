### Interest Only Loans kya hai?

Interest-Only Loan mein aap kuch samay tak sirf byaaj bhugtan karte hain. Iska matlab hai ki aapka monthly outflow kam hota hai.
Benefits - Shuruat mein payments kam hote hain. Aapke paas adhik financial flexibility hoti hai shuruat ke varshon mein.
Cons - Higher Total Cost: Long term mein aapko adhik byaaj dena pad sakta hai. Principal Amount: Maturity par ek bada lump sum amount chukana padta hai. Long-term financial planning ke hisaab se soch-vichar zaroori hai.
