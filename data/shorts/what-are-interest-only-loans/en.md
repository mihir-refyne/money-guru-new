### Interest Only Loans kya hai?

Interest-Only Loan mein aap kuch samay tak sirf byaaj bhugtan karte hain. Iska matlab hai ki aapka monthly outflow kam hota hai.
Pros:
- Shuruat mein payments kam hote hain. Aapke paas adhik financial flexibility hoti hai shuruat ke varshon mein.
Cons:
- Higher Total Cost: Long term mein aapko adhik byaaj dena pad sakta hai. Principal Amount: Maturity par ek bada lump sum amount chukana padta hai. Yadi aap temporary financial relief chahte hain toh yeh ek vikalp ho sakta hai, lekin long-term financial planning ke hisaab se soch-vichar zaroori hai.
