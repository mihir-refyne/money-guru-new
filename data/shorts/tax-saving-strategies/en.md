### What is Tax Savings and How you can do it too?

You have to pay taxes, but there are ways to save money on them. 
- You can do this by finding things you can subtract from what you owe and things that give you extra money. 
- You can also put money in special accounts that help you save on taxes. 
- It's a good idea to talk to someone who knows a lot about taxes to get the best advice.
