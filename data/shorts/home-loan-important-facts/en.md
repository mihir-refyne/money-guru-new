### Home loan lene se pehle ye jaan le!

- Apne financial stability ko jaanchein.
- Different banks aur institutions ke interest rates compare karein.
- Hidden charges aur loan ki terms aur conditions samjhein.
- Down payment aur EMI ki capability evaluate karein.
- Insurance par bhi vichar karein.
