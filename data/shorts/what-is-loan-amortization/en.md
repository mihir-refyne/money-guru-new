### Loan Amortization kya hota hai?

Loan Amortization, yani loan repayment schedule, batata hai ki aapka loan kaise chukta hai samay ke saath.
- Har mahine jo aap bhugtan karte hain, uska ek hissa principal amount mein jata hai aur ek - byaaj mein. Shuruaat mein zyadatar byaaj chukana padta hai.
- Waqt ke saath, principal amount mein adhik bhugtan hota hai. Yeh schedule aapko har payment ke baad baki loan amount ka clear idea deta hai.
