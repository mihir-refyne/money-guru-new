### Your credit card can damage your credit score. Know how you should use credit cards.

The ideal limit you should use on your credit card is generally no more than 30% of your credit limit. Using a higher percentage of your credit limit can damage your credit score and indicate to lenders that you may be an overspender. Keeping a low credit utilization rate and paying balances in full and on time can help maintain a healthy credit score.
