### Is your credit card damaging your credit score? Know how much you should use credit cards.

The ideal limit you should use on your credit card is generally <b>no more than 30%</b> of your credit limit. Using a higher percentage of your credit limit can damage your credit score and indicate to lenders that you may be an overspender. Keeping a low credit utilization rate and paying balances in full and on time can help maintain a healthy credit score.
