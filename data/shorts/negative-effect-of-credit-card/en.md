### Credit cards can damage credit score. Tips for Credit cards

The ideal limit you should use on your credit card is <b>no more than 30%</b> of your credit limit. Using a high percentage of your credit limit can damage your credit score and indicate to lenders that you may be an overspender. Keeping a low credit utilization rate and paying balances in full and on time can help maintain a healthy credit score.
