### Is your credit card damaging your credit score? 

The ideal limit you should use on your credit card is no more than 30% of your credit limit. Using a high percentage of your credit limit can damage your credit score and indicate to lenders that you may be an overspender. Keeping a low credit utilization rate and paying balances in full and on time can help maintain a healthy credit score.
