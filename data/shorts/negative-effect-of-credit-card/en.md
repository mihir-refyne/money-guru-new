### Is your credit card negatively affecting your credit score? Know how much you should spend on credit cards

The optimum limit you should use on your credit card is generally no more than 30% of your credit limit. Using a higher percentage of your credit limit can negatively impact your credit score and indicate to lenders that you may be overextended. Keeping a low credit utilization rate and paying balances in full and on time can help maintain a healthy credit score.
