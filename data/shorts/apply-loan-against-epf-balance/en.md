### How can I apply for a loan against my EPF balance?


To apply for a loan against your EPF balance, you need to submit a loan application form to your employer or the EPFO office. The loan amount depends on your EPF balance and the specific rules. Once approved, the loan amount will be disbursed to bank account. You can repay the loan in monthly installments through deductions from your salary.
