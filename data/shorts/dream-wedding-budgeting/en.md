### Dream Wedding Budgeting

The loan amount for your marriage expenses should be determined based on your financial situation and ability to repay. Cover only essential expenses through the loan and limit total debt to no more than 40% of your annual income. Also, be aware of foreclosure charges and prepayment penalties.
