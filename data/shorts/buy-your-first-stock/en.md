### Pehla Stock Kaise Kharidein?

- Research Karo: Pehli baar nivesh karne se pehle company aur industry ki achhi research karo.
- Demat Account Kholo: Stock khareedne aur bechne ke liye Demat aur Trading account ki zarurat hoti hai.
- Budget Tay Karo: Kitne paise nivesh karna hai, pehle decide karo.
- Risk Samjho: Har nivesh mein risk hota hai, isey samjho aur phir nivesh karo.
- Long-Term Socho: Stock market mein nivesh ek long-term game hai, patience rakho!
