### Pehla Stock Kaise Kharidein?

- Pehli baar nivesh karne se pehle company aur industry ki achhi research karo.
- Stock khareedne aur bechne ke liye Demat aur Trading account ki zarurat hoti hai.
- Kitne paise nivesh karna hai, pehle decide karo.
- Har nivesh mein risk hota hai, isey samjho aur phir nivesh karo.
- Stock market mein nivesh ek long-term game hai, patience rakho!
