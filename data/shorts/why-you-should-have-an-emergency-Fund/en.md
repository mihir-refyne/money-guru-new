### Why you should have an Emergency Fund

Having an emergency fund is essential to deal with any  unforseen situation.

- Determine how much you want to save.
- Automate your savings.
- Prioritize saving for 3 to 6 months of expenses.
Be prepared for any emergency and secure your future and that of your loved ones.
