### Repo Rate and Home Loans: How Interest Rates Impact Your EMIs

Repo rate is the interest rate at which central banks lend money to commercial banks. <b>When it increases, lenders may increase home loan interest rates, leading to higher loan EMIs</b>. For example, a repo rate increase from 5% to 6% may increase a Rs 50 lakh home loan EMI by Rs 1,162 per month.
