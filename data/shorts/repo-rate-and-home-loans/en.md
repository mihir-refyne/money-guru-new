### When are you financially ready to buy your dream home?

Repo rate is the interest rate at which central banks lend money to commercial banks. When it increases, lenders may increase home loan interest rates, leading to higher loan EMIs. For example, a repo rate increase from 5% to 6% may increase a Rs 50 lakh home loan EMI by Rs 1,162 per month.
