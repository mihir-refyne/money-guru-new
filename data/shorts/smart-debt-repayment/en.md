### Loan Repayment: How to Pay Multiple Loans and Save More!

When faced with multiple loans, prioritize paying loans with higher interest rates, such as credit cards or loans. If struggling to make minimum payments, allocate any extra funds toward high-interest loans. For example, If you have a credit card debt with 20% interest and a student loan with 8%, prioritize paying the credit card debt first
