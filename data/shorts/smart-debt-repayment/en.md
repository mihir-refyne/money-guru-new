### Smart Debt Repayment: How to Prioritize Multiple Loans and Save Money!

When faced with multiple loans, prioritize paying off loans with higher interest rates, such as credit cards or payday loans. If struggling to make minimum payments, allocate any extra funds toward high-interest loans. For example, If you have a credit card debt with 20% interest and a student loan with 8%, prioritize paying off the credit card debt first.
