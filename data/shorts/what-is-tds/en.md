### TDS: Automatic TAX Deduction

- Tax Before You Get: Aapke income se pehle hi kuch tax kaat liya jata hai, jise TDS kehte hain.
- Various Sources: Salary, rent, interest etc. par TDS lagu hota hai.
- TDS Certificate: Year end par aapko TDS certificate milta hai jise aap tax returns file karte waqt use kar sakte hain.
