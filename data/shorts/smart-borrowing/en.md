### Are you Borrowing more money than you can repay?

<b>Smart borrowing rule:</b> Do not live beyond your means, Borrow only what you can easily repay.
- Car EMIs should not exceed <b>15% of net monthly income.</b>
- Personal loan EMIs should not exceed <b>10% of net monthly income.</b>
- Total monthly loan payments should not be more than <b>50% of the monthly income.</b>
