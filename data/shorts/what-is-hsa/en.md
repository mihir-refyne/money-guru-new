### HSA: Health Insurance Aur Savings Ek Saath!

- Health Savings Account (HSA) tax-free saving aur spending account hai.
- Medical expenses ke liye istemal hota hai, tax ka fayda bhi milta hai.
- High-deductible health plans (HDHP) ke saath use hota hai.
- Yeh long-term savings ke liye bhi upyogi hai, retirement tak.
- Contributions, earnings, aur withdrawals par tax nahi lagta.
