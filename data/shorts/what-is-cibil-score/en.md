### What is CIBIL Score? How can it affect your money?

CIBIL score is a number between 300 to 900 showing how good you are at repaying money. A higher number means a better credit score. It plays an important role in deciding your eligibility for loans, credit cards, etc.

A high CIBIL score of more than 800 increases the chances of getting approved for credit, better interest rates and terms.
