### What is CIBIL Score and why is it crucial for financial wellness?

CIBIL score is a number that shows how good you are at paying back borrowed money. It ranges from 300 to 900; the higher the number, the better your credit score. It plays a crucial role in determining an individual's eligibility for loans, credit cards, and other financial products. A high CIBIL score increases the chances of getting approved for credit and may also result in better interest rates and terms.
Good CIBIL score should be more than 800 which is accepted by all banks.
