### Are you financially ready to buy your dream home?

Financial readiness to buy a home depends on factors like stable income, strong credit score, manageable debt, and a saved down payment. 
For example, if you want to buy a Rs 80,00,000 home, you should have 20% i.e. 16,00,000 saved for the down payment. <b> Considering long-term goals is also crucial before making a decision. </b>
