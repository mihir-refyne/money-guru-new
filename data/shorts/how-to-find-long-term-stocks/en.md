### Long Term Stocks kaise dhunde?

- Market research aur analysis se shuruaat karein.
- Company ke financial health aur stability par nazar daalein.
- Future growth prospects dekhein.
- Dividend history aur management ke quality ko evaluate karein.
- Na keval price, balki value par focus karein.
