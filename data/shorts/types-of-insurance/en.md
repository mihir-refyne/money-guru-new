### Different Types of Insurance You Should Consider

Insurance is like a safety net that helps you if something bad happens. You can get different types of insurance to help protect you in different situations, like if you get sick or if something happens to your car or home. 
It's important to think about what you need and what might happen to you, so you can choose the right insurance for you.
