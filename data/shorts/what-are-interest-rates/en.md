### Interest Rates: How do they Impact Your Money?

Ever wondered how 'interest rates' affect your financial life?
- They are the cost of borrowing money or the return on your savings.
- High rates? Your loans cost more but your savings grow faster.
- Understanding rates can help you make better financial decisions.
- Remember, it's not just about rates, but how you manage them.
