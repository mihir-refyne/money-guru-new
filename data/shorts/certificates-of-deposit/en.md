### Higher Intertest Returns with Certificates of Deposit

Certificates of Deposit (CDs) are like savings accounts that allow you to deposit money for a fixed period and earn a fixed interest rate, which is typically higher than regular savings accounts. CDs can be issued for a minimum deposit of ₹1 lakh and in subsequent multiples of it.
