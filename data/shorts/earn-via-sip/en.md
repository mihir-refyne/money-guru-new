### SIP se paise kaise kamaye? 

- Regular Investment: Har mahine thoda-thoda paisa daalein, jisse saving ka discipline aaye.
- Compounding Benefit: Time ke saath paisa grow hota hai, jisse returns bhadte jaate hai.
- Market Dynamics: Market ke ups-downs mein bhi SIP average cost benefit deta hai.
- Acchi Funds Mein Nivesh: Past performance aur low fees waale funds chune.
- Patience Pays: Invest kiye gaye paiso par vishwaas rakhein aur long-term dekhein.
