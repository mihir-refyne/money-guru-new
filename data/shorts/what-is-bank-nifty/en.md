### Bank Nifty kya hai?

- Bank Nifty, NSE ka ek special index hai.
- Yeh India ke top 12 banks ke stocks par based hai.
- Market ke trends ko samajhne mein madad karta hai.
- Jab Bank Nifty upar jaata hai, banking sector achha perform kar raha hai ka sanket hota hai.
