### Digital vs. Physical KYC

- Digital KYC: Online verification using Aadhaar, PAN, etc.
- Physical KYC: In-person document verification.
- Benefits: Digital is fast & convenient; no paperwork.
- Takeaway: Digital KYC is the future of verification!
