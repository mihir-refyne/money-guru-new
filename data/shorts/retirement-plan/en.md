### Retirement Planning: How to Save for Your Future -

Saving for retirement means putting away money now so you'll have money to live on when you're older. 
- Figure out how much you need to save, make a plan, and stick to it. 
- Start saving early and add more money over time. Ask a grown-up for help if you need it.
