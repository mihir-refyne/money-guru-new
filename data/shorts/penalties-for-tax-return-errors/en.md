### What are the penalties for tax return errors?


Penalties for tax return errors include late filing fees, interest on unpaid taxes, and penalties for underreporting income. It's important to file accurately and on time to avoid these penalties. Keep records, consult a tax professional if needed, and rectify any mistakes promptly to stay compliant with tax regulations.
