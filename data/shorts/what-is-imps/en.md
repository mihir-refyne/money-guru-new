### IMPS: Turant kre Paise Transfer

- Jab aapko turant paise bhejne ki zaroorat ho, tab IMPS best option hai.
- Anytime Access: 24/7 available hai, bank ki chhuttiyon ya weekends par bhi.
- Safety First: Secure technology ke sath, IFSC aur MMID se paise transfer karein.
