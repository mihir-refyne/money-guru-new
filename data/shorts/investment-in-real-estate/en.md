### How to grow your money using real estates?

Investing in real estate can help you build wealth over time. On buying a property, you can rent it out to generate income or sell it for a profit. Real estate can appreciate in value, meaning it may be worth more in the future than when you buy it. It's important to do your research and consult experts to make smart investment decisions.
