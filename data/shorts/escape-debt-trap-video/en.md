### Escaping the Debt Trap

If you owe money to lenders and cannot pay back your debts, you may feel trapped. To escape this situation, you can take the following steps - 
- Create a budget
- Cut Back on unnecessary expenses
- Prioritize paying loans with the highest interest 
- Seek Assistance from Loan Management Services/Credit counselling agencies
