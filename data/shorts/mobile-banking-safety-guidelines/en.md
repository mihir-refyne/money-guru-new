### Mobile Banking Safety: Protect Your Paisa!

- Use Strong Passwords: Mix of letters, numbers.
- Never share OTPs: Banks kabhi OTP maangne wale call/SMS nahi karte.
- Download only official apps.
- Check statements regularly.
- Takeaway: Safety first! Keep info private.
