### Interest aur Loan ki Shartein

- Interest rate loan cost ka ek main part hai, jo loan amount ke percent mein hota hai.
- Fixed aur variable, do types ke interest rates hote hain. Fixed rate same rehta hai, par variable rate market ke hisab se change hota hai.
- Loan terms mein repayment time, interest rate, aur doosre rules hote hain. Loan lena se pehle, interest rate aur terms samajh lena important hai.
