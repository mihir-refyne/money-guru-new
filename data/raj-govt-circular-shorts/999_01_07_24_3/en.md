### Establishment of Appellate Authorities

This statutory notification announces the establishment of appellate authorities for resolving disputes related to industrial establishments in the Japanese International Investment Zone, Neemrana and Ghiloth. These authorities will function from July 1, 2024, for a duration of six months.
